🗓 New development SOP

Every new development or big CR follows this process. The kickoff email is generated automatically from the Email Generator.

Steps

  1. Confirm scope and business requirements with stakeholder
  2. Estimate phase durations: Design, Dev/SIT, UAT, Prod
  3. Generate and send kickoff email using the Email Generator
  4. Confirm team availability — collect responses within 2 business days
  5. Create JIRA tickets for each phase and assign owners
  6. Begin Design phase — document data requirements
  7. Confirm data is ready in L6P before Dev/SIT starts
  8. Complete Dev/SIT — get sign-off before moving to UAT
  9. Run UAT — stress test with multiple data runs
  10. Get UAT approval from business stakeholder
  11. Migrate to Production — confirm date with business first
  12. Send feedback survey using the Email Generator

Key rules

  • Any delay in UAT = new timeline + new CPS sent
  • Data must be in L6P before Dev/SIT can start — no exceptions
  • Prod migration date must be approved by business in writing

🧪 UAT process

User Acceptance Testing confirms the development works correctly with real data before going to production.

Prerequisites

  • Development completed and signed off
  • Data available in L6P and Portals
  • Sufficient data for stress testing and multiple runs
  • Team available for validation (see timeline email)

During UAT

  1. Run initial test with baseline dataset
  2. Document any defects in JIRA immediately
  3. Retest after each fix — do not batch fixes
  4. Run stress test with full production-scale data
  5. Get sign-off from business stakeholder

UAT approval

Written approval (email or Teams message) from the business stakeholder is required before migrating to production. Save the approval confirmation in the project SharePoint folder.

🚀 Production deploy

Pre-deploy checklist

  • UAT approval received and documented
  • Production date confirmed with business
  • Rollback plan documented
  • IT notified if infrastructure changes needed
  1. Send migration confirmation email to stakeholders (24h before)
  2. Execute migration on agreed date
  3. Run smoke test immediately after migration
  4. Confirm with stakeholder that production is working
  5. Update JIRA tickets to Done
  6. Send feedback survey within 1 business day

⭐ Feedback process

After every project delivery, we send a satisfaction survey. Results help us improve every engagement.

  • Send within 1 business day of going live — use the Email Generator
  • Follow up if no response within 5 business days
  • If any score is 1–3, schedule a brief call to understand why
  • Log results in the project SharePoint folder
  • Share learnings at the next Weekly Ops Sync
